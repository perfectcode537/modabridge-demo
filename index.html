Chapter 06 / Hang tags

Questions brands
ask first.

How is ModaBridge different from Trendsi?

Trendsi gives you access to a shared wholesale catalog. We make products for your brand only: your designs, your fabrics, your labels, so customers can't find the same item in other stores.

What is your minimum order quantity?

It depends on the product and fabric, but we work with factories that accept small first runs so new brands can test the market without big risk.

Can I get a sample before production?

Yes. Every project starts with a sample. You approve fit, fabric and finish before we start the full production run.

How long does production take?

A typical timeline is sampling first, then production, then shipping. We give you a clear schedule in your quote so you can plan your launch.

Do you handle shipping to the US?

Yes. We arrange air or sea freight to your address, warehouse or 3PL, and share tracking the whole way.

I only have an idea, not a design. Can you help?

Absolutely. Send reference photos or a rough sketch and we help you build the tech pack and develop the product.
